# Hunt fund

A group whose members keep each other accountable for bedtime and daily steps.

## Dates

**Sleep night**:
The night-anchor calendar date for sleep, from 8:00 PM that evening through 6:00 AM the next morning. Activity at 1:00 AM on Thursday belongs to Wednesday's sleep night.
_Avoid_: night, step night

**Step day**:
A calendar day's step total, including the hours before 8:00 PM. Wednesday's steps are Wednesday's.
_Avoid_: step night, night

## Stakes

**Penalty**:
A rate that covers a member from an effective date: an amount per half-hour past bedtime, or per 500 steps below that day's step target.
_Avoid_: charge

**Charge**:
The amount owed for one sleep night or one step day, in the unit of the penalty that covers that date.
_Avoid_: penalty
